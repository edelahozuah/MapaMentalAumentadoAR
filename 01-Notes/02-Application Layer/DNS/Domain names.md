#review2 

- Ways of identifying resources on the Internet intended for **people**.
	- Mnemonic: easy for people to remember and understand.
	- A name is the concatenation of strings of labels separated by dots.
- They identify:
	- A host within a domain
	- In general, a resource.
- Example: www.aut.uah.es
	- The host www is within the domain aut.uah.es, which in turn is in the domain uah.es, which is part of the TLD (Top-Level Domain) of Spain (.es).
