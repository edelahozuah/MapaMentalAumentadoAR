- Cookies are used as a {{“generic” mechanism for identifying users.}}
- They make it possible to identify the computer–browser combination.
- Procedure used by many websites.
	- To “track” their users' activity.
		- E.g., advertising sites, e-commerce sites, etc.
	- In principle, to provide better, personalized service…
		- E.g., for authorization, shopping “carts”, etc.
	- Very controversial!!! In a way, it violates users' privacy.
		- It allows “things” to be known about users.
			- Name, address, e-mail, consumption habits, content visited, how many times, in what order, etc.
			- It enables “behind-the-scenes” business: advertising companies can exchange information, etc.
