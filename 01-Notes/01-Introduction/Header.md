- The header, or control information, is {{the portion of information used by each protocol to implement the service offered to the upper layer.}}
- In a communication, each layer protocol in a **transmitter** {{adds or concatenates}} its header to the [[Service data unit (SDU)]] received from the upper layer. In contrast, each entity in the layers of a receiver {{processes and then removes}} its respective header from the [[Protocol data unit (PDU)]] (which is the information received from the lower layer).
- The header is formally known as {{PCI (Protocol Control Information)}}.
