The ICI enables {{the exchange of information between two adjacent layers, where the upper layer describes the different parameters the lower layer needs in order to perform the desired service.}}
For example, when we want to send a parcel through a courier company, in addition to the parcel itself (which would be the [[Service data unit (SDU)]], in computer network terms) the user must fill in a form with a series of fields such as the sender, the recipient, and how the parcel should be handled, such as 24-hour or 48-hour delivery, with or without confirmation, etc. This kind of information is what would be exchanged through the ICI.
